Adopted 18.05 grants Battle-shock after Emergency setup. Its end-of-turn
qualification applies to charge ineligibility, not to Battle-shock. The shared
Battle-shock state controls OC, Stratagem targeting and Actions and persists until
the existing authorized clear owner resolves it.

The native regression begins at an explicit wounded Wave Serpent/cargo checkpoint.
An equipped Lord Exultant pistol makes the actual public attack, destroys the
carrier, resolves cargo hazards and reaches an accepted public Emergency placement.
On the accepted Section17 engine the surviving Dire Avengers remained absent from
the shared Battle-shock state. This is a reproduced 18.05 supported boundary
failure, independent of full roster/capacity-provider or prior-attack admission.
The original red receipt and all precondition/fixture attempts remain outside the
source tree in the author packet.

Ordinary and attached Emergency producers already share `record_move_completion_event`.
That owner now routes their accepted Emergency occurrence through the existing
direct Battle-shock mutation before completion rules and OC preparation. Rejected
placements and other disembark modes do not enter this path. No Battle-shock test
or outcome trigger is invented. The existing `unit_disembarked` event supplies
the corresponding historical reconstruction, with exact accepted decision closure
and physical living-model authority. Existing Combat status ownership is retained.

Two incoming tests asserted the obsolete Emergency metadata value `end_of_turn`.
Their complete original bytes and specific correction rationale are preserved in
the author packet. Only that value is corrected to `until_cleared`; charge-lock
expiry, hazards, casualties, geometry, removal ordering and replay assertions stay.
Legacy non-Emergency `destroyed_transport` mode is outside the adopted 18.05 path.

Companion Terrain Objectives, same-point marker limits, the current Deadly
Demise/Fight-on-Death decision and all inherited source/provider/solver/full-suite
qualifications remain unchanged. Altered-history hardening remains separately
queued under the sequential review policy. No global rules or full-game claim.
