# SF04-002 and SF04-003: attack completion boundaries

This slice separates consumed Shooting selection, completed model attacks and
completed unit attacks. The adopted Section04 source is the retained maintained
mirror, not a new live GW observation. Operative blocks are
`rule:04:04.03.04:1` blocks2/3 and `rule:04:04.03.05:1` block2; the independent
review pins their SHA256 values to
`f760ee7e3ce11ab4f7d3ec58d36e410caf3629e1a5e38fde86b03c98500a20a7`,
`f104b31c655f1eb2cba4aee921b7bb2570f6332aaf37937fa33061c56d45762d`,
and `ec1500b998410b5921b8cd8229270f4cfb73aab463d48393ba09be1fc42e5a66`.

`selected_unit_ids` still consumes ordinary selection at selection time. A
nonempty sequence enters `shot_unit_ids` when all attacks, including deferred
mortals, have resolved, even while target post-attack destruction choices remain.
Empty, weaponless and declined-target selections
retain their existing completion/restriction authority and never count as shots.
Firing Deck's physical cargo restriction remains recorded at declaration.
Out-of-phase ranged participation retains the parent phase and does not consume
ordinary Shooting's unit-shot inventory.

The shared executor records each bearer's completion from its physical
contributions, including inside a native gathered group. Finalized miss and failed
wound steps discharge their physical attacks before sibling choices. Every
generated hit needs its own terminal work; pending damage, transport and
bearer-owned deferred mortal wounds remain unresolved. Other selected weapons on
the same bearer also block completion. The authenticated history prefix supplies
the same roll-step evidence used by native gathered resumption; a claimed pending
inventory cannot replace missing physical steps. Whole-sequence completion, after-attack
destruction processing, Hazard/Hazardous and after-unit hooks retain their owners.
This does not change the deferred Deadly Demise finding.

Each incremental `attack_sequence_models_attacked` contains only newly completed
models, in sorted order, and immediately follows an engine sequence-state receipt.
History binds it to its original declaration, weapon/target identities and the
same completion predicate. Duplicate/foreign/missing claims fail closed; the
whole-sequence boundary requires the complete declared model union. Empty
declarations preserve one empty participation receipt. Action history and
whole-unit reactions consume the authenticated sequence union rather than assume
one participation event. Ordinary shot inventory is authenticated on restore from
the complete declared bearer union, rather than post-attack reaction completion.

The new redundant sequence-state receipt is classified under the existing RNG
history-neutral event policy, beside the whole-sequence state receipt. It adds
no random draw and does not perturb unchanged physical dice histories. Semantic
model-participation events keep their existing RNG treatment. No compatibility
projection, dice override, seed change or old-artifact migration is added.

The external decision and persistence envelopes remain unchanged. The new runtime
identity is mandatory: existing saves/replays retain their original runtime and
are not relabelled or migrated. No compatibility shim is introduced. Independent
review of the patch and its exact candidate basis precedes integration. This
slice does not authorize coherency, SF08, other deferred findings or publication.
