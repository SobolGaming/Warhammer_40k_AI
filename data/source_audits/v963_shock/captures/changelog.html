FAQ

5 answers, 10 translation-only
  • AddedFAQCan a unit be controlling more than one objective at once?

    Yes.

  • AddedFAQIf a rule's effect lasts until the end of a specific part of the battle (e.g. until the end of the phase, until the end of the turn), is that rule still in effect at the end of that part of the battle?

    Yes. For example, if an ability gave a unit +1 OC until the end of the turn, at the end of the turn, that unit would still have +1 OC. That unit would not have +1 OC at the start of the next turn.

  • AddedFAQWhen a model overhangs the edge of the battlefield, how does that affect its visibility?

    That model's controlling player can draw line of sight from any part of that model that is not overhanging the edge of the battlefield. Their opponent can draw line of sight to any part of that model.

  • AddedFAQWhen determining control over an objective, can the same model's OC count towards more than one objective that model is within range of?

    Yes.

  • AddedFAQWhen does a model's or unit's ability in an attached unit apply to all models in that unit?

    A rule that applies to 'this/that unit' or 'models in this/that unit' applies to all models in an attached unit. Rules that just apply to one model in an attached unit (for example, a rule that grants a model Feel No Pain or Lone Operative) only apply to that model, not to all models in the attached unit.
