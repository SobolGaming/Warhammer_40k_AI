# Contract 13 to 14

Contract 14 implements Order 32 continuous any-part and every-facing-part
visibility over the supported analytic model volumes. Shooting candidates and
their saved decision metadata carry a changed, engine-owned line-of-sight witness.

`LineOfSightWitness` now requires `context_fingerprint`. Each model record
requires `evidence` with algorithm identity
`continuous-analytic-prism-visibility:1`, the complete geometry fingerprint,
both visibility predicates, proof-kind tokens, a checked positive-witness count,
and optional exact corridor endpoints or hidden target part. Coordinates are
canonical rational `n/d` strings. Model-record `checked_ray_count` and
`clear_ray_indices`, and blocker-record `ray_index`, are removed. The explicit
low-level ray-query API still reports actual ray counts.

Full visibility means every self-facing target part has a clear corridor from
some self-valid observer origin. Different target parts may use different
origins. Blocker records inventory applicable geometry and policy; an individual
record does not prove terrain-caused Cover. Clients must display engine-produced
visibility and Cover and must not reconstruct them from candidate rays or blocker
counts. New proof kinds require an algorithm-identity change if their contract
changes incompatibly. An unresolved computation raises a typed error and never
becomes a rules answer.

These nested rule payloads already use open JSON fields in the request,
projection, and event schemas. Their envelope shapes and family discriminators
remain unchanged. The normative nested payload change requires this new bundle
major even though a structural schema comparison alone cannot detect it.
The affected versioned wrappers are:

- `session-metadata-v14-contract`;
- `session-command-result-v14-contract`;
- `session-command-outcome-v14-contract`;
- `session-persistence-v6-continuous-visibility`, contract `14.0.0`.

Existing finite choices and parameterized proposal shapes remain unchanged.
Public viewer scoping continues through the shared adapter redaction owner.
External session checkpoints require the exact engine build and contract identity;
replay artifacts also retain their exact build check. Raw internal lifecycle and
LocalGameSession restoration are separate surfaces: when they encounter a stored
visibility witness, its typed decoder requires the new fields, without inferring
continuous evidence from old samples. This is not a general cross-build migration
guarantee for internal payloads that contain no witness.

The reference server supports Contract 14 only. Clients regenerate their models
and discard cached Contract 13 metadata and command outcomes. Operators complete
or archive older sessions on a separately retained deployment before transferring
service authority; no checkpoint conversion or dual-major negotiation is added.
Existing deployment retention commitments remain in force. All released
compatibility baselines remain immutable; the new baseline is
`compatibility/14.0.0-shape.json`.

Order 33 Indirect Shooting rule changes are outside this migration.
