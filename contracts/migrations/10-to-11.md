# Contract 10 to Contract 11 migration

Contract 11.0 makes physical ranged-weapon copy identity mandatory throughout
the shooting declaration and resolution contract. Every engine-emitted ranged
weapon availability row has a deterministic `weapon_instance_id`, and every
`WeaponDeclaration` must submit that exact identifier. Accepted declarations,
attack pools, decision and event records, persistence artifacts, and replay
round-trips retain it unchanged.

This is a deliberate major-version break. A Contract 10 shooting declaration
identifies only the attacking model, wargear, profile, and optional Firing Deck
source. That tuple cannot distinguish two identically equipped copies. Contract
11 does not infer a missing identifier, assign declarations to copies by array
order, or treat an omitted identifier as copy one.

## Changed payload families

The changed family identities are:

- `https://warhammer40k-core.local/contracts/v2/proposal-payload.schema.json`,
  whose `shooting_declaration.declarations` entries require
  `weapon_instance_id`;
- `parameterized-submission-v2-weapon-instances` and the corresponding `/v2/`
  schema URI;
- `session-command-envelope-v2-weapon-instances` and the corresponding `/v2/`
  schema URI;
- `interaction-conformance-v3-weapon-instances` and the corresponding `/v5/`
  schema URI;
- `session-metadata-v11-contract`, `session-command-result-v11-contract`, and
  `session-command-outcome-v11-contract`, whose schema URIs move to
  `contracts/v11/`; and
- `session-persistence-v3-weapon-instances` and its `/v3/` schema URI.

Finite submissions, create-session input, lifecycle status, decision request,
projection, event-delta, battlefield, rules-catalog, capability, and replay
artifact families are otherwise unchanged. Their generic JSON-bearing fields
may carry the new engine-authored weapon instance identifier without changing
their existing structural discriminator.

## Shooting declarations

Clients must treat `weapon_instance_id` as an opaque engine-owned identifier.
They must copy it from the selected `available_weapons` row into the matching
declaration and must not derive it from display names, wargear order, or profile
text. Distinct equipped copies have distinct IDs even when their model, wargear,
profile, and target are identical.

Each physical copy can select any otherwise-legal target independently. Two
distinct copies may select the same target or different targets. Submitting the
same physical-copy/profile/Firing-Deck-source declaration key more than once is
invalid and rejects before the pending request is popped or authoritative state
is mutated. Catalog-defined independently selectable multi-profile groups such
as C'tan Powers may expose distinct legal profiles that share a physical-copy
ID; clients may declare those distinct rows subject to the engine-emitted
selection limits. For Firing Deck, the ID belongs to the embarked source
model's equipped weapon copy; it is not a new weapon owned by the Transport.

Accepted attack pools retain one identifier per declared copy. Grouped attack
resolution may gather otherwise-identical pools for dice processing, but its
contributing pool records retain the individual weapon instance IDs for audit
and replay.

## Persistence and replay

The Contract 11 persistence root requires
`external_contract_version: "11.0.0"`, the v2 command envelope, and v11 command
outcomes. Recovery accepts only `session-persistence-v3-weapon-instances` and
does not upgrade a v2 artifact or synthesize identity for a historical Contract
10 shooting command. Operators must complete or archive Contract 10 sessions on
the retained 10.x deployment before moving authority to Contract 11.

The replay artifact discriminator remains `replay-artifact-v8-phase17n-step5a`.
New Contract 11 exports preserve weapon instance identity in their JSON-safe
decision, event, and lifecycle payloads. A replay containing a Contract 10
shooting declaration without the required identity remains bound to the
retained Contract 10 runtime; Contract 11 does not reinterpret it.

## Client migration

Clients upgrading from Contract 10 must:

1. regenerate models from the Contract 11 OpenAPI document and canonical JSON
   Schemas;
2. require `weapon_instance_id` on every ranged availability row and copy it
   into every shooting declaration;
3. key local weapon-allocation state by the engine-emitted physical weapon
   instance together with its profile and optional Firing Deck source binding;
4. use the v2 parameterized-submission and session-command-envelope wrappers
   and the v11 metadata/result/outcome families;
5. preserve weapon instance identity when displaying accepted attack pools,
   decisions, events, and replay data; and
6. discard cached Contract 10 pending requests, command envelopes, interaction
   conformance cases, metadata, outcomes, and persistence artifacts instead of
   attempting an in-place upgrade.

Deployers must retain a separately deployed 10.x adapter through at least
2027-08-23 and one released 11.x minor line, whichever is later. The retained
adapter is a separate deployment pinned to a 10.x build; the Contract 11
reference server does not provide content negotiation or parallel Contract 10
endpoints. Prior retention dates remain in force for deployments covered by
earlier migrations.
