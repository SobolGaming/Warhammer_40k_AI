# Contract 21 to 22: committed flight and turn movement history

Before every Normal Move, Advance, Fall Back or Charge, clients choose the
engine-issued walking or Take to the Skies option. Advance and Charge rolls
follow that finite choice. Hover retains both options and waives only the two-inch
maximum-distance penalty. The penalty follows Charge roll modifiers and any
source cap; it never changes the dice result. Flight permissions apply only to
FLY models within the selected rules unit.

Movement retains its existing `movement_mode` contract. Charge selection composes
`:take_to_the_skies` with the existing unit/modifier-ignore option IDs. Charge and
reactive choices include explicit `take_to_the_skies`, sorted
`flying_model_instance_ids` and `flight_penalty_inches`. ChargeRollRequest and
ChargingUnitSelection persist the boolean; completed Charge `fly_charge_policy`
contains it. Reactive proposal/reroll contexts retain the original finite
selection through retries. Clients never add a flight choice to a path proposal.

GameState now requires `model_movement_history`: per-model distances, source
event IDs, battle rounds and turn player IDs. Accepted path witnesses supply the
distances, including committed model-specific vertical treatment. Heavy sums the
current turn even after MovementPhaseState has been cleared. This internal ledger
is not a viewer projection; existing shared redaction applies to emitted choices
and movement events.

Metadata and command results/outcomes advance to `v22-contract`, replay to
`replay-artifact-v16-flight-history`, and operator persistence to
`session-persistence-v14-flight-history`. Deployments support major 22. Keep old
checkpoints and replays with their matching deployment; missing flight choices
and movement evidence cannot be inferred. Released compatibility baselines remain
immutable.
