# Contract 44.3.25

Gathered attack pools now produce all original Hit rolls before resolving Hit
choices and before any Wound rolls. The Wound step prepares every eligible
attack, including its Lethal choice, then produces the original Wound rolls
before resolving Wound choices. Shared engine journals preserve physical roll,
generated-hit and accepted-choice identities through resume, restore and replay.
This correction does not claim closure of grouped Save timing or every pool
grouping interpretation.

Combat disembark requires engine-owned evidence that no legal whole-unit Tactical
setup exists. A verified Tactical witness rejects Combat. A continuous joint-pose
exclusion of a necessary domain may establish impossibility; finite candidate
search failure alone cannot. An unresolved proof returns typed invalid status
without consuming the pending request or changing state, events, histories or RNG.
The owner rechecks the same authority before hazards. Actual battlefield, terrain,
attached inventory and current rules context are used by positive validation.
Necessary-domain relaxations remain explicit: the bounded supported-terrain and
FRAME controls do not prove every legally impossible Tactical scene is decidable.

Grouped Combat hazard events now include
`disembark_payload_kind: "rules_unit_combat_disembark"`. A shared typed decoder
checks complete group placement, cargo, states, rolls and counts, and requires a
canonical round trip. Pending hazard context comparison rejects Boolean/number
aliases and unknown fields before mutation. Fight history uses the same decoder.
Standalone Combat and Emergency retain their typed routes; no compatibility shim
infers a grouped variant from an old untagged draft payload.

Existing finite and parameterized decision families, typed invalid envelopes and
viewer scoping remain unchanged. Runtime identity changes: sessions and replays
require their producing runtime. Earlier draft histories and failed captures
remain historical evidence and are not migrated or claimed restored here.
