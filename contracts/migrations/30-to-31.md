# Contract 30 to 31: Firing Deck passenger shooting restrictions

Order 65 snapshots every unit embarked when Firing Deck resolves, including
units contributing no weapons. All remain ineligible to shoot until that turn
ends, including after disembarking or moving to another Transport.

Firing Deck declaration requests require `firing_deck_embarked_unit_instance_ids`;
other units omit the field.
The accepted event's existing `ineligible_unit_instance_ids` now means all cargo.
The operator state carries a source-linked persistent effect with the exact
cargo snapshot, Transport ID and declaration result ID. Restore authenticates
its subjects and duration against accepted declarations and completed turn
boundaries. Phase-local `shot_unit_ids` contains actual shooters only. Do not
infer a complete snapshot from old contributor-only events or reuse old saves.

Finite choices and the parameterized submission envelope remain unchanged.
Metadata and command result/outcome families advance to v31, replay to
`replay-artifact-v25-firing-deck`, and persistence to
`session-persistence-v23-firing-deck`. Only major 31 is supported. Keep the 30.x
deployment for its saves/replays through at least 2027-09-20 and one released
31.x minor line, whichever is later. Earlier retention obligations and immutable
compatibility baselines remain in force.
