# Contract 36 to 37: phase-start revival coherency

Contract 37 authenticates friendly revival anchors against physical presence at
the recorded start of the actual phase, including its battle round and turn owner.
Models returned or set up later in that phase cannot become new anchors.
Current poses and retained battlefield presence still govern distance.

Battlefield revival requests and accepted healing events require
`revival_phase_start`: source rule ID and package hash, game ID, battle round,
turn owner, phase, phase-opening event/window IDs, canonical target unit ID and
sorted model IDs. `HealingEffect.phase_start_model_ids` must agree with that
historical inventory. Missing, forged or stale evidence fails closed.

Persistence advances to `session-persistence-v29-revival-phase-start`, replay to
`replay-artifact-v31-revival-phase-start`, and external wrappers to v37.
Submission envelopes and viewer permissions are unchanged. Do not synthesize
phase-start anchors from current placements in old saves; use their original
engine release. Exact engine identity remains mandatory.

Retain 36.x through at least 2027-09-24 and one released 37.x minor line, whichever
is later. Released compatibility baselines remain immutable.
