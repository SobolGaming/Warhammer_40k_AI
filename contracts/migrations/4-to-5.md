# Contract 4 to Contract 5 migration

Contract 5.0 gives the Phase 17N projection and replay additions distinct
payload-family identities. Contract 4 clients cannot safely accept these
responses because their closed schemas reject the new turn-start terrain
snapshot and terrain-classification members.

Clients must regenerate models from Contract 5 OpenAPI and schemas, then:

1. require `game-view-v7-phase17n`; each
   `primary_unit_terrain_turn_start_snapshots[].unit_memberships` collection is
   viewer-scoped to the unit identities present in that projection's
   `unit_display_by_id`;
2. require `battlefield-view-v2-phase17n`, including the authoritative
   `classification` of every terrain area and terrain feature;
3. require `session-projection-v3-phase17n`, `session-metadata-v5-contract`,
   `session-command-result-v5-contract`, and
   `session-command-outcome-v5-contract` on the corresponding session
   responses;
4. consume new replay exports as `replay-artifact-v3-phase17n` under the
   `/contracts/v3/replay-metadata.schema.json` schema ID;
5. discard cached Contract 4 projections and projection checkpoints because
   both projection family identity and viewer-scoped hashes change;
6. use a separately deployed 4.x adapter for clients that cannot migrate
   during the published support window.

The engine replay importer has one deliberate compatibility path for published
`replay-artifact-v2-phase18i` files. If their initial lifecycle state lacks the
Phase 17N `primary_unit_terrain_turn_start_snapshots` member, it is initialized
to an empty list and the artifact is loaded as v3. This represents the absence
of evidence in a format that predated the field; it does not reconstruct
historical terrain membership. Unknown replay versions and malformed v2
lifecycle/state objects fail with `ReplayArtifactError`.

There is no HTTP content negotiation between contract majors. Request payloads
continue to require their exact declared family version before engine mutation.
