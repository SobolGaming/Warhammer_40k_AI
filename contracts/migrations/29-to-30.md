# Contract 29 to 30: reserve deadlines and ingress lifetimes

Order 64 applies the Core round-two arrival minimum and round-three unarrived
reserve deadline. Accepted ingress and battlefield departure history establish
the deadline exemptions, including the route followed by embarked cargo.
Final-turn reserve cleanup is independent of those exemptions.

The existing finite movement options and parameterized placement envelopes remain
in use. An ingressed unit cannot make another type of move until the next Charge
phase starts; ordinary and reactive movement share this restriction. Cargo does
not acquire its carrier's movement lock. Repositioning preserves same-turn
Advance, Fall Back and Disembark history and applicable persisting effects.

Operator state now requires `is_ingress` on each phase movement record and
`destroyed_at_end_of_battle` on each reserve state. The first is reconstructed
from accepted completion evidence, not trusted as an independent assertion.
The second selects the independently sourced final-turn destruction boundary;
the original declaration/departure deadline policy remains intact. These fields
must not be inferred for old saves. Existing viewer scoping still applies.

Replay advances to `replay-artifact-v24-reserve-lifetimes`, persistence to
`session-persistence-v22-reserve-lifetimes`, and metadata and command
result/outcome families to `v30-contract`. Only major 30 is supported. Retain the
29.x deployment for old saves/replays through at least 2027-09-19 and one released
30.x minor line, whichever is later. Earlier retention obligations and immutable
baselines remain in force.

Authenticated loaded-Transport reconstruction still replays from the independent
pre-ingress origin. A dedicated versioned performance workload covers restore and
fork after ingress, Rapid Disembark and a later accepted decision, without reducing
the authentication work or introducing a cache that bypasses validation.
