# Contract 34 to 35: Normal Move phase occurrences

Contract 35 requires `turn_player_id` on every `NormalMoveState`. This is the
player whose turn contains the phase, separately from `player_id`, the moving
unit's owner. The once-per-phase identity is battle round, turn player, phase,
unit owner and rules-unit identity. Reactive Normal Moves during an opponent's
turn do not consume a unit's Normal Move in its own turn.

Triggered movement completion payloads now include the descriptor's canonical
`movement_mode`. The shared completion recorder records Normal Moves, and restore
checks those records against retained accepted decisions and completion events.
Missing, ambiguous or drifted occurrence evidence is invalid. Do not infer a
turn owner from unit ownership to migrate a checkpoint.

Persistence advances to `session-persistence-v27-normal-move-occurrence`, replay
to `replay-artifact-v29-normal-move-occurrence`, and external wrappers to v35.
Old checkpoints/replays require their matching engine release. There is no
compatibility fallback. Decision envelopes and viewer visibility are unchanged.

Keep the 34.x deployment available through at least 2027-09-23 and one released
35.x minor line, whichever is later. Released compatibility baselines remain
immutable. Regenerate engine identity, the new major baseline, external examples
and the TypeScript client, and run the exact PR-base check and installed wheel
smoke before publishing.
