# Contract 38 to 39: witnessed deemed base contact

Order 85 implements Core 01.04.04. Runtime model geometry preserves explicit,
source-linked body parts separately from the rules base. The normal base range
and engagement measurements keep their existing meaning.

Accepted witnessed movement against body geometry records a `base_contact_query`
in its path result. Qualifying pairs additionally record `deemed_base_contacts`,
including the pinned source, original movement context and counterfactual path
without the enemy overhang. Persistent per-model movement history carries the
required `base_contacts` array, including when empty. Restore authenticates the
pair, physical history, actual witness, budget, endpoint constraints and source;
it never reconstructs a missing contact query from permissive defaults.

The shared adapter redactor removes full feasibility queries and counterfactual
paths from viewer payloads. Operator persistence and exact replay retain them.
Public contact entries identify the source and enemy model only. No new decision
family or adapter-local contact selection is introduced.

Metadata and command result/outcome families advance to v39. Operator persistence
uses `session-persistence-v31-deemed-contact`; replay uses
`replay-artifact-v33-deemed-contact`. Old saves require their original exact
runtime; no missing body/contact authority is inferred. Retain 38.x through at
least 2027-09-25 and one released 39.x minor line, whichever is later.
