# Contract 7 to Contract 8 migration

Contract 8.0 replaces the physical-unit-only Primary terrain history with an
authoritative, group-aware turn-start position history. Each snapshot now uses
`rules_unit_memberships`; an attached rules unit is one outer row containing
strict physical component rows. Component rows record the exact evaluated
models, logical terrain areas, and objective-marker/model witnesses. The old
`primary_unit_terrain_turn_start_snapshots` property is removed and the required
replacement is `primary_rules_unit_turn_start_snapshots`.

This is a deliberate major-version break. Contract 8 does not infer grouped
history from Contract 7 physical rows and provides no compatibility alias or
default. The replay state also adds required, engine-private battlefield
departure evidence and destruction attribution fields. Old replay artifacts are
rejected before restoration.

The changed payload-family identities are:

- `game-view-v10-phase17n-step3`;
- `battlefield-view-v4-phase17n-step3`;
- `decision-request-view-v4-phase17n-step3` and the corresponding
  `contracts/v5/decision-request-view.schema.json` URI;
- `lifecycle-status-v3-phase17n-step3` and the corresponding
  `contracts/v3/lifecycle-status.schema.json` URI;
- `session-projection-v6-phase17n-step3`;
- `event-delta-v3-phase17n-step3` for in-process integer cursors and
  `event-delta-v4-phase17n-step3` for authenticated HTTP cursors;
- `session-metadata-v8-contract`, `session-command-result-v8-contract`, and
  `session-command-outcome-v8-contract`; and
- `replay-artifact-v6-phase17n-step3`.

The live decision-family schema URI advances to
`contracts/v6/decision-family-live.schema.json` because its definitions inherit
the new decision-request family. All other request and response family versions
are unchanged. In particular, the Contract 7 directed per-player Primary
assignment shape remains required.

Clients upgrading from Contract 7 must:

1. regenerate models from the Contract 8 OpenAPI and JSON Schemas;
2. replace the removed physical terrain-history property with the grouped
   turn-start position property and consume complete public group rows;
3. discard cached projections, event cursors, projection hashes, session
   metadata, and replay checkpoints before reconnecting; and
4. obtain a fresh authenticated projection before submitting another command.

Army-list unit/model identities and post-reveal turn-start history are public
to both players, including units that remain unplaced in Strategic Reserves.
Frozen starting attachment records are likewise public mustering evidence:
Contract 8 does not treat Leader or Support attachment bindings as hidden
Declare Battle Formations state. Unresolved reserve, transport-cargo, and
faction-rule formation choices and their derived state remain viewer-scoped
until reveal.

Declare Battle Formations secrecy remains attached to the declaration choices
and unrevealed declaration state, not roster identity. Event-time source and
destroyed objective witnesses likewise describe public battlefield facts, so
player and administrator streams receive identical evidence. These visibility
rules are part of the Contract 8 semantic break.

When Declare Battle Formations is present in the setup sequence, its unresolved
interval begins at game creation and includes mustering; an opponent therefore
does not receive another player's reserve or faction-setup
request/result/events, embarked-unit manifest, Dedicated Transport setup
consequence, reserve/cargo state, or model pose during that interval. A setup
sequence without that step has no such secrecy interval. Administrators and the
owning player retain the authoritative view. The public
`battle_formations_revealed` event then publishes the deterministic final
formation state, and every later battlefield projection is public to both
players. Clients must use that reveal boundary instead of inferring declarations
from missing deployment options or raw battlefield-state siblings.

The nested battlefield-view family changes from
`battlefield-view-v3-phase17n` to `battlefield-view-v4-phase17n-step3` because
its model-state projection now enforces that same pre-reveal viewer boundary.
Its coordinate system and geometry unions are unchanged.

The Contract 8 replay loader accepts only
`replay-artifact-v6-phase17n-step3`. It does not migrate
`replay-artifact-v5-phase17n` snapshots, invent missing objective proximity,
infer a destroying unit from a player ID, or synthesize battlefield-departure
history.
