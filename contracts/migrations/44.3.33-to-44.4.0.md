# Contract 44.4: controlling-player coherency casualties

Adds the finite `select_coherency_casualty` family through the existing model
entity selection and finite submission envelopes. Options enumerate every placed
model across the controlling player's broken rules units and belong to that player.
Actor, request, option, payload and current geometry/group drift are rejected
before mutation. The native turn-end flow pauses and rechecks coherency after each
accepted casualty, including attached physical components.

The existing cleanup/decision/event containers retain per-casualty receipts and
the final cleanup completion. Cleanup payloads require a typed `completes_boundary`
marker; identifiers remain opaque and missing historical markers are not inferred.
A new `coherency_casualty_required` pending scoring
window binds valid saves to the native queue while preserving earlier frozen
control. No destroyed-model reaction is emitted; physical mission casualty
evidence remains authoritative. Request, submission and persistence envelopes
are unchanged. Exact runtime matching remains required; no old artifact converter
or compatibility fallback is introduced. See
[bounded source and scope](../../docs/SF03_001_COHERENCY_CHOICE_SCOPE.md).
