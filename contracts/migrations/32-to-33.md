# Contract 32 to 33: roster model selections

Order 68 requires `model_profile_id` and positive one-based `model_index` on
every WarlordSelection and EnhancementAssignment. The index addresses the
immutable source unit's models of that profile in deterministic model-ID order.
It is an explicit selection, including for single-model units. Validation rejects
missing profiles, indexes outside the selected composition, non-Character ordinary
bearers and Epic Hero bearer models. Upgrades permit Character and non-Character
units; existing source-specific restrictions still apply.

RuntimeEnhancementAssignment additionally records `bearer_model_instance_id`.
The original physical identity follows authenticated split lineage and remains
bound after death; it never transfers to the first surviving model. Warlord grants
belong to that selected model, with rules-unit keywords derived from presence.

Player army lists advance to `core-v2-player-army-list-v2-model-bearers`.
The versioned example lists explicitly retain their previous engine-selected
model identities. Other lists require explicit bearer selection during migration.
There is no missing-field inference or automatic conversion of old saves.

Replay advances to `replay-artifact-v27-roster-models`, persistence to
`session-persistence-v25-roster-models`, and metadata/command result families to
v33. Only major 33 is supported. Retain the 32.x deployment for its saves/replays
through at least 2027-09-20 and one released 33.x minor line, whichever is later.
Previous retention obligations and compatibility baselines remain in force.
