# Contract 5 to Contract 6 migration

Contract 6.0 publishes `battlefield-view-v3-phase17n`. Every authoritative
terrain-area entity now requires `logical_terrain_area_id`, matching the
required field in mission-setup terrain placements. Isolated physical
footprints use their own `terrain_area_id`; physical footprints identified by
source rules as one terrain area share a distinct logical ID.

The changed nested battlefield shape gives `GameView` the
`game-view-v8-phase17n` identity and `SessionProjection` the
`session-projection-v4-phase17n` identity. Session metadata, command result, and
command outcome responses use their `v6-contract` identities; metadata's
existing `server_contract_version` value and validation pattern move from 5.x
to 6.x. Because create-session requests and replay artifacts can embed a
mission setup, their discriminators advance to `create-session-v2`,
`session-create-v3`, and `replay-artifact-v4-phase17n` as appropriate.

Clients must regenerate models from the Contract 6 OpenAPI and schemas, then:

1. require `game-view-v8-phase17n`, `battlefield-view-v3-phase17n`, and
   `session-projection-v4-phase17n`, then read
   `authoritative.terrain_areas_by_id[*].logical_terrain_area_id`;
2. render each physical `footprint` independently while applying terrain-area
   rules to all members carrying the same logical ID;
3. stop inferring logical grouping from polygon proximity, contact, labels,
   colors, or terrain-area identifiers;
4. emit `create-session-v2` directly, or `session-create-v3` for the
   authenticated protocol, and include `logical_terrain_area_id` on every
   embedded terrain-area placement;
5. emit and accept `replay-artifact-v4-phase17n`; do not infer logical grouping
   while upgrading v2 or v3 artifacts—route them to the retained 5.x
   deployment;
6. require the `v6-contract` session metadata, command-result, and
   command-outcome response identities;
7. discard cached Contract 5 battlefield projections and geometry hashes,
   because logical identity now participates in `authoritative_geometry_hash`;
8. fetch a fresh authenticated projection before submitting further commands;
9. use a separately deployed 5.x adapter for clients that cannot migrate
   during the published support window.

The HTTP API does not negotiate between contract majors. Request payloads
require their exact family versions before engine mutation. Contract 5
create-session payloads, replay artifacts, and cursors remain valid only against
the retained 5.x deployment.
