# Contract 24 to 25: oversized setup authority

Mission setup now requires nullable `attacker_battlefield_edge` and
`defender_battlefield_edge` fields. Both must be absent (`null`) or distinct
canonical cardinal edges or the source corner tokens described below. Source layouts supply and
validate them. Custom layouts need explicit edges to permit oversized deployment;
`null` remains legal for ordinary deployment but cannot authorize that exception.
The fields are present in configuration, persistence, replay and viewer mission
projections. They contain public mission information.

Existing deployment and prebattle placement proposals permit an oversized base
only after the engine proves it cannot fit in its deployment zone at any
translation or orientation, and validates contact with its player's battlefield
edge. Diagnostics include `large_model_player_edge_unsupported` and
`large_model_edge_contact_missing`. All ordinary placement limits remain active.
No new proposal kind or finite option family is needed.

Strategic Reserve exception proposals retain their existing per-model edge
records. AIRCRAFT exceptional models are exempt from the oversized setup-turn
activity lock. A non-exempt exceptional model restricts its entire rules unit.
The existing arrival event and ReserveState restriction fields carry the result.
Movement, reactive movement, shooting and charge now enforce that record, and
stale movement submissions fail before queue pop. End-turn cleanup expires it.
Pregame setup does not invent a first-turn restriction. Restore rejects forged
restriction evidence. No hidden information is introduced.

Metadata and command results/outcomes advance to `v25-contract`, persistence to
`session-persistence-v17-large-model-setup`, and replay to
`replay-artifact-v19-large-model-setup`. Only major 25 is supported by this runtime.
Keep the preceding 24.x deployment through at least 2027-09-17 and one released
25.x minor line, whichever is later. Earlier retention obligations remain.
Old artifacts require their original deployment; missing edges or restrictions
are not repaired by a compatibility fallback. Released baselines are immutable.

Source corner edges (`north_west_corner`, `north_east_corner`,
`south_west_corner`, `south_east_corner`) designate their two adjoining
cardinal edges. Contact with either designated edge satisfies the source corner
edge; contact with an opposing edge does not. Source layout identity is retained.
