# Contract 39 to 40: source-linked random profile characteristics

Order 87 introduces `value_kind: "random"` for source-linked model and weapon
characteristics. Such a value contains `characteristic`, `expression` (quantity,
sides, intrinsic modifier) and `source_id`. Engine-bound modifiers are separate
from the expression. A resolved occurrence additionally contains `evaluation`
and `evaluation_id`; these fields must occur together. Catalog descriptors are
unresolved and unmodified. Clients must never treat missing numeric fields as
zero, a dash, or permission to roll locally.

Random ranged weapons retain `kind: "distance"`, add `random_value`, and carry
`distance_inches: null` until evaluated. Selection commits each physical weapon's
Range; declaration retries and target replacement retain that roll. An accepted
unit selection can finish without a legal target after its ranges are determined.

Datacard projections add `random_expression`. Before an evaluation their numeric
fields are null and their display string is the expression. Projections consume
engine state without rolling. A skipped Hit roll may have a null target; an
automatic Wound may have null Strength, Toughness and target. A Battle-shocked
model with random OC contributes zero without evaluating its suppressed source;
its contribution's `objective_control` is null and effective OC is zero.

Uninitialized random-Wounds roster models carry null `starting_wounds` and
`wounds_remaining`. They become live models only when the engine initializes
health. Live damage/healing use that health; a later W characteristic evaluation
for save grouping does not heal or damage the model. During a datasheet handoff,
physical starting and remaining health persists through the replacement descriptor.

Operator persistence retains `random_weapon_ranges` when nonempty, and the
source-linked model/weapon evaluation events and their physical dice evidence.
Restore validates source, owner, occurrence and recorded value; it does not roll.
Skipped Dark Pacts Leadership tests use a null `leadership_target`.
Existing finite selections and parameterized proposal families remain the only
player-choice paths. The adapter must not submit a random value or alter its
source descriptor.

Metadata and command result/outcome families advance to v40. Game views use v13,
session projections v9, persistence `session-persistence-v32-random-profiles`,
and replay `replay-artifact-v34-random-profiles`. Older saves require their exact
original runtime. Retain 39.x through at least 2027-09-25 and one released 40.x
minor line, whichever is later; there is no history-inference migration.
