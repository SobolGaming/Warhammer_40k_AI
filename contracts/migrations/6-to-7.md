# Contract 6 to Contract 7 migration

Contract 7.0 replaces the single mission-wide `primary_mission_id` with the
required `primary_mission_assignments` array. The array contains exactly two
strict objects. Each object requires `player_id`, `force_disposition_id`, and
`primary_mission_id`, and represents the Primary Mission resolved for that
player from the ordered player-versus-opponent Force Disposition matrix cell.
Both assignments are public mission data. Reversing the player and opponent
Force Dispositions may select a different Primary Mission and must not be
collapsed into one shared value.

This is a deliberate major-version break. Contract 7 has no compatibility
field, inferred default, or migration fallback for the Contract 6 scalar.
Payload producers must resolve and submit both directed assignments. Engine
validation remains authoritative for player coverage, Force Disposition
identity, matrix resolution, and roster consistency.

The affected payload-family identities are:

- `create-session-v3` and `session-create-v4`;
- `decision-request-view-v3-primary-assignments` and
  `annotated-decision-request-v2-primary-assignments`;
- `interaction-conformance-v2-primary-assignments`;
- `event-delta-v2-primary-assignments` for in-process event cursors and
  `event-delta-v3-primary-assignments` for authenticated HTTP event cursors;
- `lifecycle-status-v2-primary-assignments`;
- `game-view-v9-phase17n` and `session-projection-v5-phase17n`;
- `session-metadata-v7-contract`, `session-command-result-v7-contract`, and
  `session-command-outcome-v7-contract`;
- `capability-manifest-v2-directed-primary` and
  `support-profile-v4-directed-primary`;
- `physical-proposal-context-v2`; and
- `replay-artifact-v5-phase17n`.

The `battlefield-view-v3-phase17n` family is unchanged. Directed Primary
Mission assignments do not change authoritative battlefield coordinates,
logical terrain-area identity, or geometry hashes.

Clients must regenerate models from the Contract 7 OpenAPI and schemas, then:

1. replace every emitted or cached mission-level `primary_mission_id` with the
   exact two-entry `primary_mission_assignments` array;
2. treat both assignments as public mission state and preserve both in create,
   projection, event, reconnect, replay, and capability-manifest flows;
3. require the new create, decision, lifecycle, projection, support, session,
   physical-context, event, and replay discriminators listed above;
4. include both assignments when calculating or comparing public mission,
   projection, event, replay, capability, and selection identities;
5. discard Contract 6 mission projections and event cursors, capability manifests, replay
   metadata, and checkpoints, then fetch a fresh authenticated projection
   before submitting another command; and
6. route Contract 6 replay artifact v4 payloads and clients that cannot migrate
   to the separately retained 6.x deployment.

The Contract 7 replay loader accepts only `replay-artifact-v5-phase17n`. It does
not infer two assignments from a Contract 6 `replay-artifact-v4-phase17n`
scalar. Replay `source_identity` additionally requires `mission_pack_id` and
`mission_source_package_hash`. They are an atomic pair: both are non-null for a
configured or late-bound mission setup, and both are null only when the replay
snapshot has no mission setup. The loader resolves the canonical mission pack
and rejects source-package hash drift. The HTTP API likewise does not negotiate
between contract majors, and mismatched request family versions fail before
engine mutation.
