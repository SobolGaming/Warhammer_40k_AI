# Contract 37 to 38: interpreted dice results

Order 84 implements Core 01.05.07 and 01.05.08 through the shared dice and
lifecycle decision authorities. Contract 38 requires new explicit dice history
fields; it does not migrate old replay or checkpoint records.

- `DiceRollOverrideRecord.component_index` is a required integer for a physical
  die assignment or `null` for an aggregate roll assignment. Original and rerolled
  physical faces remain bounded. Assigned positive integer results can exceed
  those bounds. `current_values` contains assigned component values; an aggregate
  assignment preserves the components and changes the unmodified total.
- `DiceRollComponent` keeps its physical `value` and carries a required nullable
  `result_override`; its effective value follows that authenticated record.
  `DiceRollInstance.result_override` carries aggregate assignment evidence.
  `UnmodifiedRollResult.result_override` preserves both assignment scopes before
  ordinary modifiers and result limits. These nullable fields are required even
  when no assignment occurred.
- `select_dice_extremum` is a finite die-selection decision. The effective active
  player chooses an engine-enumerated component ID. Its request binds the source
  reference, original roll, complete current dice state, highest/lowest kind,
  round, phase, turn owner and visibility. Ties are never broken by array order.
- `dice_extremum_referenced` and `dice_extremum_selected` retain request/source
  and selection authority. Private dice use the shared player-owned event and
  decision redaction. Adapters never choose, replace or roll dice locally.

Metadata and command outcome/result families advance to v38. Operator
persistence advances to `session-persistence-v30-dice-results`. The existing
finite submission, projection and event envelopes retain their shapes. New
sessions use 38.0.0 and its exact engine-build identity; old sessions continue
on their matching 37.x deployment. Keep 37.x through at least 2027-09-24 and one
released 38.x minor line, whichever is later.

Hit and Wound payloads require `critical_is_threshold`: false means the default
exact-six rule; true means a source-granted inclusive threshold. Hit payloads also
require `success_requires_exact` for ordinary Snap Shooting. Restore authenticates
these flags with the owning recorded Hit context. Tied-die contexts additionally
require `reference_id`, identifying the source occurrence independently of retries.

Replay artifacts use `replay-artifact-v32-dice-results`; v31 histories require replay
with their original runtime and must not be relabeled as v32.

Tied-die contexts require `clock_event_id` and `scope_request_ids` for historical
chooser authentication. The event stream includes `out_of_phase_shooting_started`
and `fight_activation_completed` scope evidence. Out-of-phase shooting declaration
acceptance includes its `attack_sequence_id` to identify the corresponding scope
completion. These are required v38 history semantics within the existing envelope.
