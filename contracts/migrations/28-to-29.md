# Contract 28 to 29: reserve Transport cargo and Rapid Disembark

Order 63 permits a loaded Strategic Reserve Transport to ingress as one unit.
Cargo stays embarked, counts toward the reserve points limit, and cannot ingress
independently. Rapid Disembark validates every passenger against the Transport's
effective ingress placement policy, including source-conditioned regions and
minimum distances. An invalid placement retains cargo and permits a fresh retry.

The finite movement options and parameterized placement envelope are unchanged.
Clients supply passenger placements, never the inherited policy. Arrival events
retain typed `ingress_placement_restrictions` as operator-only authority; public
projections and events strip it through shared redaction. Loaded Transport
ingress also retains an operator-only `ingress_placement_history_origin`. Restore
re-executes accepted decisions from that independent pre-ingress boundary, so
matching forged event and policy copies cannot alter inherited restrictions.

Replay advances to `replay-artifact-v23-ingress-placement-history`, persistence to
`session-persistence-v21-ingress-placement-history`, and metadata and command
result/outcome families to `v29-contract`. Only major 29 is supported. Retain the
28.x deployment for its old saves/replays through at least 2027-09-19 and one
released 29.x minor line, whichever is later. Earlier retention obligations and
immutable baselines remain in force. No legacy policy is inferred during load.

Order 64 owns the separate arrival/deadline defaults and general post-ingress
activity lifetimes. This migration does not certify those clauses.
