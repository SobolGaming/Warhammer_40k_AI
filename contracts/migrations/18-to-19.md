# Contract 18 to 19: canonical Charge actors and per-model endpoints

Contract 19 implements Order 47 (C11-04). Clients select an Attached Unit's
canonical actor once and submit complete paths for its living models across all
physical components. Component aliases cannot be used as separate Charge actors.
Each model must move closer to a selected target and satisfy the within-one-inch
and engagement obligations whenever a legal path is possible. A failed finite
search rejects explicitly instead of supplying an exemption.

Charge result events now require `endpoint_witness.model_endpoints`. Each sorted
row identifies the model, its physical component, the stable reviewed source,
per-target distances, engagement/preferred target sets, and typed reachability
evidence. Old aggregate summaries remain derived output. These are engine-owned
records, not new client-supplied choices. Existing finite and parameterized
submission envelopes and viewer-redaction ownership are unchanged.

Metadata and command result/outcome schemas advance to `v19-contract`.
Operator persistence advances to `session-persistence-v11-charge-model-endpoints`
and replay to `replay-artifact-v13-charge-model-endpoints`. Restored evidence must
match historical physical ownership, inventory and geometry. Regenerate clients
and fetch fresh session state; discard cached actor IDs and Charge proposals.

The reference server supports major 19 only. Contract 18 checkpoints and replay
artifacts require their matching deployment; missing model evidence is never
inferred. Released compatibility baselines remain immutable. Existing obligations
to retain older hosted deployments remain in force under the compatibility policy.
