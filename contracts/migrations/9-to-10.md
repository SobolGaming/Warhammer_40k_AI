# Contract 9 to Contract 10 migration

Contract 10.0 publishes the Phase 17N Step 5A Primary scoring-state replay
contract. Every `GameState` lifecycle payload now requires
`objective_control_record_authorities` and
`primary_scoring_state_evidence_records`. Both registries are engine-owned;
scoring-state rows are content-addressed snapshots of the authoritative state
consumed at an ordinary or end-of-battle Primary scoring boundary.

This is a deliberate major-version break. A Contract 9 replay has neither
registry, so it cannot prove which Primary progress, Action/departure history,
frozen unit-destruction history membership, and current group-aware physical
memberships backed each applicable Primary evaluation. Contract 10 does not treat a
missing registry as empty, infer evidence from the final battlefield, or trust
only the evidence ID/hash copied into a victory-point transaction. Contract 9
artifacts remain valid only against the separately retained 9.x deployment.

The changed payload-family identities are:

- `replay-artifact-v8-phase17n-step5a` and the corresponding
  `contracts/v8/replay-metadata.schema.json` URI; and
- `session-metadata-v10-contract`, `session-command-result-v10-contract`, and
  `session-command-outcome-v10-contract`, whose schema URIs move to
  `contracts/v10/` because metadata declares the Contract 10 server major.

All request families, decision/lifecycle/event families,
`game-view-v11-phase17n-step4`,
`session-projection-v7-phase17n-step4`, and
`battlefield-view-v4-phase17n-step3` are unchanged. The Objective Control
authority and scoring-state evidence registries are replay/audit state and are
deliberately omitted from viewer projections. Their addition does not change
redaction, coordinate, geometry-hash,
event-cursor, or projection-hash semantics.

## Required Objective Control authority

`initial_lifecycle.state.objective_control_record_authorities` is required,
even when its value is an empty array. Every persisted Objective Control record
has exactly one closed `objective-control-record-authority-v1` row. The row
binds the record ID and SHA-256 content hash to:

- one closed, content-addressed `primary-mission-boundary-checkpoint-v1` with
  exact model presence, wounds, model-placement JSON, source/resolved Objective
  Control, terrain membership, Attached Unit state, phase flags, active marker
  and prior-use state, and typed Objective Control modifier-source provenance;
- the exact retained sticky-control states applied to the record; and
- deterministic authority and checkpoint IDs/hashes.

Restore recomputes record membership, characteristic authority, sticky-control
application, and source inventory from this frozen checkpoint. It never uses a
later battlefield position or treats a persisted Objective Control result as a
new trust root.

## Required scoring-state evidence

`initial_lifecycle.state.primary_scoring_state_evidence_records` is required,
even when its value is an empty array. Each row is closed and contains:

- discriminator `primary-scoring-state-evidence-v1`, game, battlefield,
  battle-round, active-player, phase, and objective-control timing;
- `scoring_boundary_kind`, exactly `ordinary` or `end_of_battle`, so a final
  objective-control record may support two distinct scoring boundaries;
- objective-control record identity and content hash;
- the complete Primary Mission progress state frozen at that boundary;
- the qualifying assigned-Primary Mission Action history and battlefield
  departure history;
- the exact sorted Primary unit-destruction state IDs whose history was
  available to that scoring boundary;
- current rules-unit position witnesses, each with owner identity and the
  existing group-aware rules-unit membership shape; and
- frozen per-player spatial-condition evidence, including requested condition
  IDs, table-quarter unit witnesses, enemy units wholly within owned territory,
  and opponent-territory objective IDs; and
- deterministic evidence identity and SHA-256 content hash.

The registry is exactly inverse-complete over assigned-Primary rules. Every
Objective Control record with at least one rule applicable to its timing has
exactly one row for each required `ordinary` or `end_of_battle` boundary kind,
including a row when deterministic evaluation awards zero VP. Conversely,
every row must map to an applicable-rule boundary. Awarded transactions must
equal deterministic re-evaluation, so deleting transactions together with
their evidence does not erase the required historical boundary.

The schema reuses the existing closed Primary progress, Mission Action,
battlefield-departure, destruction-state identity, and rules-unit membership definitions. The runtime also
validates ordering, uniqueness, source authority, content hashes, objective
control, score references, event relationships, and boundary context. JSON
Schema acceptance alone is not replay certification.

## Loader and session behavior

The Contract 10 runtime accepts only
`replay-artifact-v8-phase17n-step5a`. It rejects a v7 discriminator before
restoration and rejects a v8 state that omits either registry, omits any row
field or applicable-rule boundary, adds an unknown row or field, drifts nested
state without updating its content address, persists evidence for an
inapplicable boundary, or updates an award's evidence ID/hash without the exact
persisted row. No backwards-compatibility shim or shape inference is provided.

Session metadata now emits `server_contract_version: "10.0.0"` with
`session-metadata-v10-contract`. Command results and idempotent command outcomes
embed that metadata and therefore advance to their own `v10-contract`
wrappers. Command requests, session-create requests, reconnect projections,
event deltas, and their validation paths are unchanged.

Clients upgrading from Contract 9 must:

1. regenerate models from the Contract 10 OpenAPI and JSON Schemas;
2. accept only `replay-artifact-v8-phase17n-step5a` from a Contract 10 server
   and preserve the complete required Objective Control authority and
   scoring-state registries when storing an artifact;
3. use the v10 metadata/result/outcome wrappers and require a compatible 10.x
   `server_contract_version`;
4. keep viewer rendering on the unchanged game-view/session-projection
   families and do not expect the replay-only registries in a live projection;
5. discard cached Contract 9 replay metadata and replay checkpoints instead of
   attempting to upgrade them in place; and
6. route historical v7 replay artifacts to the retained 9.x deployment.

Deployers must retain that separately deployed 9.x adapter through at least
2027-08-16 and one released 10.x minor line, whichever is later. Prior
retention dates remain in force for earlier migrations.
