# Contract 44.3.23

Core Counter-offensive may target an otherwise eligible friendly unit during the
opponent's Fights First step before granting that unit Fights First. The shared
eligibility query retains its ordinary band-selection default; Core discovery,
target validation and effect application explicitly request eligibility before
the grant. Presence, previous selection, attached lineage, legal fight types,
opponent-turn timing, CP, Battle-shock and atomic request-context checks remain.

Existing parameterized Stratagem requests, activation continuation, viewer/event
projections and persistence/replay schemas cover this correction. No field,
decision family or snapshot conversion is added. Sessions and replays remain
bound to their exact producing runtime.
