# Contract 44.3.31

Heavy now checks setup and cumulative accepted movement independently of the
movement action. An admitted short Advance can receive its source-defined +1
in the owning player's Shooting phase when the unit is unengaged, was not set
up this turn, and every applicable model moved at most three counted inches.
Assault supplies separate Advance shooting permission; Heavy grants none.

Stationary/Indirect semantics, physical ownership, movement measurement,
decision requests, proposal/event/viewer/persistence schemas and replay format
remain unchanged. Existing saves and replays require their matching runtime.
No old-runtime converter or broader Core, provider, faction or full-game
certification is supplied.
