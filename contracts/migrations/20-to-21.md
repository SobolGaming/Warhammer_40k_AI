# Contract 20 to 21: shared Heroic Intervention Charges

Heroic Intervention starts a source-authorized Charge using the ordinary finite
declaration, modifier-ignore and declaration-grant options, whole-roll rerolls,
target selection/replacement and witnessed movement proposals. Clients select
the engine's emitted options in order. The mode is mandatory in the Stratagem
proposal before CP spending and rolling.

Into the Fray records a source-linked `roll_limit` of six in the shared movement
budget. Apply that limit after Charge roll modifiers and before separate movement
distance effects. Other Charges encode `roll_limit: null`. Leap to Defend binds
its targets to successful Charge moves in the suspended opponent phase.

The engine-private Charge state now records an optional `interruption` containing
source use IDs, authorized actor, target restrictions and the suspended completed
phase. Start/completion events preserve that source and the resolved child phase.
A temporary `charge` active-player scope identifies the acting player while turn
ownership remains unchanged. Fights First expires at the current turn's end.
Restore validates the source use, roll limits, owner and completion history.

The old Heroic-only movement context, completion events and
`heroic_intervention_reroll_context` are removed. Existing shared Charge events,
proposal diagnostics, reaction continuation, viewer scoping and finite submission
envelopes apply throughout.

Metadata and command results/outcomes advance to `v21-contract`, replay to
`replay-artifact-v15-interrupted-charges`, and operator persistence to
`session-persistence-v13-interrupted-charges`. Major 21 is supported by this
deployment. Old checkpoints and replays require their matching deployment;
missing Charge source history is never inferred. Released compatibility baselines
and previous deployment retention obligations remain unchanged.
