# Contract 35 to 36: revived-model engagement

Contract 36 permits a returned model to engage any model in a canonical enemy
rules unit that was already engaged with the receiving rules unit immediately
before placement. Attached components share one unit identity; retained destroyed
models count while rules-present. Phase-start coherency anchors are unchanged.

HealingEffect no longer serializes `phase_start_enemy_engagement_model_ids`.
Accepted battlefield revival events carry source-bound `revival_engagement`
evidence with canonical enemy unit IDs before placement and those engaged by the
returned model. Recovery authenticates those sets against physical history and
requires exactly one mutation per accepted placement decision. Legacy model-scoped
effects, missing evidence and drifted evidence are rejected. Do not map an old
model ID to its current unit to migrate history; use its original engine release.

Persistence advances to `session-persistence-v28-revival-engagement`, replay to
`replay-artifact-v30-revival-engagement`, and external wrappers to v36. The finite
and parameterized submission envelopes and viewer policy retain their shapes.

Retain 35.x through at least 2027-09-24 and one released 36.x minor line, whichever
is later. All released compatibility baselines remain immutable.
