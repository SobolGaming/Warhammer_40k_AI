# Contract 11 to 12

Contract 12 implements Order 30 / P05B. Fight On Death selection now occurs
before physical removal and before the model's other destruction triggers.
Acceptance retains the original fixed placement until its unit has attacked
or the phase ends. Deferred destruction can pause for normal engine decisions;
the original cause completes once after its children finish.
Shoot On Death uses the same retained destruction record and executes through
the ordinary out-of-phase shooting proposal path, restricted to the destroyed
model. A source-authorized shoot-or-fight grant consumes exactly one selected
action. Shooting cleanup follows that model's attacks; Fight cleanup follows
its unit's normal activation or the phase boundary.

Clients must:

1. Continue selecting one emitted option on `select_destruction_reaction` through
   the finite submission API. A retention request contains applicable retained
   attack sources and decline; source-authorized alternatives have separate
   `:<action>` option IDs and an `action` payload. Select an emitted ID verbatim.
   Later ordinary reactions have a separate request. Submit the subsequent
   `submit_shooting_declaration` through the existing parameterized API using
   only its engine-enumerated weapons and target witnesses.
2. Render retained models using their existing placement. They remain targets
   and sources for measurement, visibility, datasheet abilities and Stratagems.
   They remain enemy Engagement and collision geometry. They cannot move or
   receive new damage. Use engine-emitted target inventories and movement
   witnesses rather than deriving them from zero-wound status.
3. Read `present_model_instance_ids` in Fight movement target-authority witnesses
   in place of `placed_living_model_instance_ids`. Movement actor witnesses
   still contain only living models. These target records use their existing JSON envelopes.
4. Consume `fight_on_death_retention_selected` and
   `fight_on_death_destruction_completed` for public retention/removal updates.
   Read `selected_action` to distinguish shooting and fighting. Shooting also
   emits `retained_shooting_attacks_completed` and source-authorized
   `retained_shooting_hazardous_automatically_passed` events. Opening, trigger,
   suspension, `retained_shooting_started`,
   `hazardous_destruction_routing_started` and progress records remain engine-private.
   Do not depend on cause IDs or continuation hashes in viewer payloads.
5. Start sessions with the Contract 12 engine build. Persistence and replay
   require that exact build; obsolete `fight_on_death_awaiting_attack` effects
   and remove/re-add events are rejected. No automatic historical conversion
   or compatibility fallback is provided.

The server supports Contract 12 only. Contract 11's immutable compatibility
baseline remains committed, while `compatibility/12.0.0-shape.json` anchors the
new major. Session metadata, command results and command outcomes advance to their v12
family discriminators. Operator persistence advances to
`session-persistence-v4-retained-destruction` with
`external_contract_version: "12.0.0"`. The replay family remains
`replay-artifact-v8-phase17n-step5a`; its exact engine identity prevents
cross-build execution. Regenerate client models and discard cached Contract 11
metadata, outcomes and pending commands. Operators must complete or archive old
sessions on the retained 11.x deployment before switching authority; the new
server does not negotiate both majors.

The build includes pinned source-backed For the Chapter! and Unending Fidelity
consumers. Their prior-action restrictions and Hazardous exception are structured
source permissions. `attack_sequence_models_attacked` records participation at
the common completed-pools boundary, before deferred destruction or Hazardous
tests. This is not certification of complete Space Marines or Grey Knights
catalog fieldability.
