# Contract 17 to 18: Charge budgets and committed targets

Contract 18 implements Order 46 (C11-01/C11-03). Ordinary Charge rolls retain
their raw 2D6 and source-linked modifier trace. The modified roll is bounded
to 1–12; subsequent move-distance effects produce a separate, nonnegative
`movement_budget.maximum_distance_inches`, which can exceed 12 or be fractional.
Target eligibility still requires both the current movement maximum and the
12-inch Charge target limit.

After the roll, clients answer the new finite `select_charge_targets` request
before receiving a `charge_move` proposal. Choose an engine-issued target-set
option or the offered `decline_charge_targets` option. Nonempty path submissions
must use exactly the committed targets. Existing source-required target rules
continue to constrain the choices.

Later changes are evaluated against the original dice. If committed targets
become invalid, the shared `select_target_replacement` family resolves a new
target set or decline before movement. If the targets remain valid, the engine
refreshes movement authority without a new target choice. Stale submissions
reject before queue pop; advancing the session refreshes stale requests.

Charge roll payloads now require `movement_budget`; Charge phase state requires
`target_selection` (null before selection or after resolution). Charge proposals
carry the original `charge_roll`, current `movement_budget`, and committed
`target_selection`. `charge_targets_selected` binds each commitment to its
decision request/result. `charge_movement_request_withdrawn` records refreshes.
Internal physical-context hashes remain redacted by the shared viewer policy.
Restore authenticates target commitments and their replacement history before
allowing movement. Setup-reactive Charge uses the same bounded-roll/distance
arithmetic within its existing source-bound target context.

Metadata and command result/outcome families advance to `v18-contract`.
Operator persistence advances to `session-persistence-v10-charge-targets` and
replay exports to `replay-artifact-v12-charge-targets`. The finite submission
and parameterized submission envelopes are unchanged. Regenerate clients and
fetch fresh session data; cached Charge contexts cannot be reused.

The reference server supports major 18 only. Existing checkpoints and replay
artifacts require their matching old deployment. No automatic migration,
missing-budget inference, or adapter-owned target selection is provided. Released
compatibility baselines remain immutable.
