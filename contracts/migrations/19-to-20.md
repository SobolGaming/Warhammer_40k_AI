# Contract 19 to 20: whole Charge rerolls

Ordinary Charges now offer eligible Command Re-roll after the original dice and
any declined natural reroll, before target selection or movement-budget resolution.
A Charge reroll replaces both dice before modifiers. A natural reroll that has
already replaced them prevents a second reroll.

Command Re-roll now targets the canonical rolling rules unit. Its option binding
and opportunity target IDs use that unit, including when the roll belongs to one
of its models. The dice ID remains in the target specification and source context.
The existing CP, Battle-shock, same-Stratagem and same-unit phase restrictions
apply. Heroic Intervention has already spent its unit's phase target allowance;
its natural Charge reroll remains available without additional CP.

Clients render the engine's finite choices and submit their emitted option IDs.
Discard cached targetless Command Re-roll options. Ordinary Charge reroll requests
bind the selected action through `charge_action_id`; natural Heroic Intervention
rerolls bind their source use and original dice through
`heroic_intervention_reroll_context`. `charge_roll_started` provides the suspended
ordinary Charge's original dice authority. Existing JSON-safe finite request,
event and viewer-scoped projection envelopes are unchanged.

Metadata and command results/outcomes advance to `v20-contract`, replay to
`replay-artifact-v14-charge-rerolls`, and operator persistence to
`session-persistence-v12-charge-rerolls`. Major 20 is supported by this deployment.
Old checkpoints/replays require their matching deployment; missing source history
and obsolete reroll prohibitions are never inferred or migrated. Released
compatibility baselines and prior deployment retention obligations remain intact.
