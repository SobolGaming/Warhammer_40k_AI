# Contract 31 to 32: Aircraft

Order 66 implements Core 23.01-23.04. Aircraft begin in Strategic Reserves,
make only ingress moves and return to Strategic Reserves at the end of their
opponent's turn. The existing mandatory boundary-sequencing protocol owns the
return; clients do not submit an Aircraft movement or invent an exit option.

The legacy Hover toggle, `hover_mode_states` and movement-policy
`hover_mode_active` are removed. Hover retains AIRCRAFT and only preserves the
existing exemption from the Take to the Skies distance penalty. Ordinary moves
and battlefield-edge exits cannot represent Aircraft departures. Old saves and
replays are rejected, including payloads that merely drop a legacy Hover field;
there is no inferred reserve history or conversion of old moves.

The public `aircraft_opponent_turn_end_departure` event binds the source rule,
turn owner, Aircraft owner, canonical/component/model IDs and resulting reserve
state. Its private mutation/departure receipts authenticate the mandatory timing
participant and exact occurrence. Repeated ingress/return cycles retain distinct
historical occurrences. Initial formation events retain shared secret redaction.

Movement availability's `enemy_aircraft_engagement_model_ids` identifies contacting models whose canonical unit has AIRCRAFT. It is separate from the individual AIRCRAFT model IDs used for transit permission.

The finite and parameterized submission envelopes are unchanged. Target lists
and eligibility reflect the new Aircraft restrictions. Metadata and command
result/outcome families advance to v32, replay to `replay-artifact-v26-aircraft`,
and persistence to `session-persistence-v24-aircraft`. Only major 32 is supported.
Keep the 31.x deployment for its saves/replays through at least 2027-09-20 and
one released 32.x minor line, whichever is later. Previous retention obligations
and released compatibility baselines remain in force.
